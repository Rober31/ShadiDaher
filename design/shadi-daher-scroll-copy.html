How it moves

The film follows the scroll
It is drawn frame by frame on a canvas and trails the scroll by about a second, so it never jerks.
The text keeps its own time
Each block plays a timed entrance when its window opens. A line rises behind a mask and settles. Nothing is tied to how fast the visitor scrolls.
The frame breathes
Your mockups show the film at four sizes: 79%, 100%, 71% and 83% of the viewport height. The film glides between them, so the pull-back before the brand cards feels like a camera move.
The white fade travels with the film
It covers the bottom 45% of the film and ends in solid white at the film’s edge, exactly as in your mockups.
The last frame holds
After the film ends the stage stays pinned a little longer, so the final button can be clicked.

Build prompt

For a new Codex project. Paste it in and attach the film, the seven screenshots, the logos, the Clash Display font and the light reference file, shadi-daher-reference.html, which holds every word of copy and every number.

SHADI DAHER · SCROLL FILM WEBSITE · NEW PROJECT

YOUR ROLE
You are a senior creative developer. You build 3D and scroll-driven websites, and you know GSAP and ScrollTrigger inside out. Work like that: plan first, keep the code small, and care about every frame.

THE GOAL
Build a one-page website for Shadi Daher, a fashion entrepreneur.
The page opens with a film that plays frame by frame as the visitor scrolls. Text appears over the film in seven sections. Under the film there are a few quiet sections with the business details.
It must feel slow, smooth and expensive. Nothing snaps, nothing bounces, nothing stutters.

WHAT I ATTACHED (use all of it)
- The film: 1920 x 814, 18 seconds, 60 fps, no sound.
- Seven screenshots: the design of the seven sections at 1728 x 1117. Match them.
- shadi-daher-reference.html: every word of copy and every number (positions, sizes, weights, timings, gradients). It is the source of truth. If it and a screenshot disagree, follow the screenshot for the look and the reference for words and timing.
- The font: Clash Display.
- The logos: Shadi Daher (header) and the five brands: Alexander, Shahd Couture, Keep it Casual, Sheikha, PregOfit.
If a file is missing, stop and ask me. Do not invent a substitute.

BEFORE YOU START
1. Check your installed skills. Use the ones that fit this job (GSAP or scroll animation, front-end design, performance) and tell me which you used.
2. Read the reference file and look at all seven screenshots.
3. Write me a short plan, 5 to 8 lines, then build.

TECH
- Vite with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework.
- GSAP with ScrollTrigger and SplitText. Lenis for smooth scrolling.
- No three.js and no WebGL. The film already carries the depth.
- Folders: /public/frames (film frames), /public/fonts, /public/logos, /src (film.js, sections.js, menu.js, styles.css), /design (my screenshots and the reference file).

STEP 1. TURN THE FILM INTO FRAMES
- Use ffmpeg (or the ffmpeg-static package) to export the film as WebP images.
- Desktop: 1920 wide, 20 frames per second. About 360 frames, about 10 MB.
- Phone: 1080 wide, 15 frames per second. About 270 frames, about 3.5 MB.
- Never scrub a <video> tag with scroll. It stutters, most of all on iPhone.

STEP 2. BUILD THE STAGE
- One full-screen section that stays pinned while the film plays: 1000vh of scroll. The first 900vh play the film. The last 100vh hold the final frame, so the last button can be clicked.
- Draw the current frame on one <canvas>. The frame follows the scroll with a soft delay (ScrollTrigger scrub about 1.2).
- Load the first frame at once. Then every 8th frame. Then the rest. Never block scrolling while frames load.
- The film is not always full-screen. It fills 79%, 100%, 71% or 83% of the screen height depending on the section, as in the screenshots. Glide between these sizes. The keyframes are in the reference file.

STEP 3. GRADIENTS
- Film fade: the bottom 45% of the film fades into white. The page under the film is white. The fade moves with the film.
- Hero: a short white fade at the top edge of the film.
- Text halo: a very soft white glow behind a text block, only where thin type needs it.
- Buttons: dark pill, gradient from #222 to #636363, white label.
- Cards: warm, see-through gradient fill, 14px corners.
Exact values are in the reference file.

STEP 4. THE SEVEN SECTIONS
The times are film seconds. Keep to them. Outside these windows the text would sit on top of the model.
1. Hero, 0 to 3. FASHION / FROM FABRIC TO BRAND. One line of text, a button (Work with Shadi) and a link (See the brands).
2. Fabric, 4.2 to 5.4. IT STARTS WITH FABRIC. One line.
3. Measure, 5.8 to 7.1. MADE TO MEASURE. One line.
4. Trust, 7.5 to 9.1. BUILT ON TRUST. One line.
5. Brands, 9.8 to 12.3. ONE HOUSE / 5 Brands. Five cards, each with a brand logo and a caption.
6. Numbers, 12.9 to 16.6. Three cards: 12 Years in fashion, 40+ Collections sold, 71K Orders shipped. Count the numbers up.
7. Next step, 17.2 to the end. TAKE THE NEXT STEP. One line and a button (Work with Shadi).
Use the copy from the reference file word for word. All text is real HTML, never part of an image.

STEP 5. MOTION
- The text is not tied to scroll speed. Each block plays its own timed entrance when its window opens, and a timed exit when it closes.
- In: each line rises from behind a mask with a slight 3D tilt and fades in. About 0.9 seconds, power3.out, lines 0.09 seconds apart.
- Out: a quick fade and a small lift. About 0.5 seconds.
- Cards rise one after another. Thin rules draw from the left.
- Open each window from the frame that is on screen, not from the raw scroll position.

STEP 6. FONT, LOGOS AND MENU
- Load Clash Display with @font-face from /public/fonts. Preload it. Use font-display: swap. The weight of every line is in the reference file.
- Use my logo files as they are. Never redraw a logo and never type a logo as text.
- The Shadi Daher logo sits top left. A three-line menu button sits top right. It opens a white full-screen panel with About, Brands, Contact and the Work with Shadi button.

STEP 7. UNDER THE FILM
Six simple sections, in this order: About, Work with Shadi, The five brands, How it works, Made in Turkey, Contact. The copy is in the reference file. White background, Clash Display headlines, generous spacing, and a soft fade-up as each section enters.

STEP 8. PHONE
- Under 768px wide the film sits at the top of the screen (about 62% of its height) and the text sits on the white area under it.
- Use the phone frames. Keep touch scrolling native.
- Brand cards in two rows. Numbers in one row.
- Set up desktop and phone separately with gsap.matchMedia.

STEP 9. QUALITY
- 60 fps while scrolling. No blank frames, no flicker, no layout jumps.
- The first frame shows in under 1.5 seconds.
- If the visitor prefers reduced motion, show seven still sections and no pinning.
- Works in Chrome, Safari (Mac and iPhone), Firefox and Edge. No console errors.

WHEN YOU FINISH
Send me screenshots of all seven sections on desktop (1728 x 1117) and on phone (390 x 844), a short list of anything that differs from my design and why, and how to run and build the project.

Check before you build

  • Two small copy fixesYour numbers card says “Order Shipped” and “+40”. I set “Orders shipped” and “40+”, which is how English readers expect them. Say the word if you want them back.
  • The weightsYou named Bold and Medium. Measured against the font, the heavy lines in your mockups are Semibold, the thin lines are Regular, the body is Light and ONE HOUSE is Extralight. The prompt lists what the mockups show.
  • The film sizeIt differs between mockups: 79%, 100%, 71% and 83% of the viewport height. The prompt has the film glide between them. If you would rather keep one size, change the stage table to a single height.
  • The type sizesBody copy is 28px in the hero and about 40px in sections 2 to 4. I kept each section as you drew it.
  • The numbers12 years, 40+ collections, 71K orders. They are live on the site now. A buyer will ask about them.
  • “Women’s fashion”Right if all five brands are womenswear. If Keep it Casual also sells menswear, drop “women’s”.
  • The trust line“Traders ask Shadi to run their orders” matches where things stand. Change it to “work with Shadi” once the first orders are running.
  • The offers and the feePrivate label, wholesale and the three steps are proposals. The traders proposed a percentage per order. Decide whether the page says so or the first call does.

Proof worth asking him for

  • How many factories he works with in Turkey.
  • Which countries he ships to.
  • How many pieces he produces in a season.
  • Typical lead time and minimum order.
  • One client story, even a short one.
  • One real photo of Shadi at a factory. The film is AI-made, and a real photo carries more weight with a trader.
