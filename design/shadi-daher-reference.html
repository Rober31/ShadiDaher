Shadi Daher build reference

Read this with the build prompt. Every word of copy and every number is here. Positions are measured from the seven design screenshots (1728 x 1117) and are given in vw and vh so they scale. Timings are measured from the film.

1. The film

  • 1920 x 814 pixels, so 2.359 to 1.
  • 60 frames per second, 17.98 seconds, no sound.
  • Never scrub a video tag with scroll. Export an image sequence and draw it on a canvas.

Export the frames.

Desktop: ffmpeg -i film.mov -vf "fps=20,scale=1920:-2:flags=lanczos" -c:v libwebp -quality 80 public/frames/d/f_%04d.webp
About 360 frames, about 10 MB.

Phone: ffmpeg -i film.mov -vf "fps=15,scale=1080:-2:flags=lanczos" -c:v libwebp -quality 78 public/frames/m/f_%04d.webp
About 270 frames, about 3.5 MB.

2. Scroll

  • One stage, 100vh tall, pinned for 1000vh of scroll.
  • The first 900vh play the film from the first frame to the last, at a constant rate.
  • The last 100vh hold the final frame, so the last button can be clicked.
  • The frame follows the scroll with ScrollTrigger scrub 1.2. No snapping.
  • Lenis for smooth scrolling, lerp about 0.09, driven by gsap.ticker and wired to ScrollTrigger.
  • One canvas, full viewport, white background, devicePixelRatio capped at 2. Redraw only when the frame or the framing changes.
  • Loading order: frame 1 first, then every 8th frame, then the rest, nearest to the current position first. If a frame is not ready, draw the nearest one that is.

8. Motion

  • Text is not scrubbed. Each block plays a timed entrance when its window opens and a timed exit when it closes, in both scroll directions.
  • Open and close each window from the frame that is actually on screen (the smoothed value), not from raw scroll.
  • In: each line rises 24px from behind a mask, with rotateX from 35 degrees to 0 (perspective 800px, origin bottom), and fades in. 0.9 s, power3.out, lines 0.09 s apart. Body copy follows 0.15 s later with a fade and a 12px rise.
  • Out: fade and lift 12px. 0.5 s, power2.in.
  • Brand cards: rise 24px and fade, 0.08 s apart. Then the rule draws from the left, then the caption fades in.
  • Number cards: the same rise. The count takes 1.4 s, power2.out.
  • Nothing bounces, overshoots or snaps.

9. Header and menu

  • Logo top left. A three-line button top right, level with the logo.
  • The button opens a full-screen white panel that wipes down from the top in 0.7 s, expo.inOut.
  • Inside the panel: About, Brands, Contact, and the Work with Shadi button.
  • Links scroll smoothly to #about, #brands and #contact. Stop Lenis while the panel is open. Esc and a close button shut it. Keep focus inside it.

11. Phone, under 768px wide

  • Stage 100svh. The film is 62svh tall from the top, cropped to cover.
  • Point of the film at the centre of the screen, per section: 47, 44, 46, 55, 55, 45, 41 (% of the film's width).
  • The same white fade.
  • Every text block sits in the white area under the film, 6vw from the edges. Headlines clamp(28px, 8vw, 40px). Body 16px.
  • Brand cards in two rows, three and two. Numbers three in a row.
  • Use the phone frames. Pin for 800svh. Native touch scrolling, no Lenis smoothing on touch.
  • Set desktop and phone up separately with gsap.matchMedia.
